# Results Reported in the IEEE Paper

## Published performance comparison

The paper reports the following comparison between the imbalanced dataset without SMOTE and the balanced dataset using SMOTE.

| Metric | Without SMOTE | With SMOTE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 89% | 97% |
| Precision | 88% | 96% |
| Recall | 91% | 97% |
| F1-score | 89% | 97% |
| ROC-AUC | 0.92 | 0.98 |

## Gaussian-noise augmentation

The paper also reports this comparison:

- Without noise augmentation: 96% accuracy and 96% F1-score.
- With noise augmentation: 97% accuracy and 97% F1-score.

## Note

These values are the results reported in the published IEEE research paper:

*Temporal Convolutional Networks for Electricity Theft Detection with Limited Data*, 19th INDIACom / 12th International Conference on Computing for Sustainable Global Development, IEEE, 2025.
