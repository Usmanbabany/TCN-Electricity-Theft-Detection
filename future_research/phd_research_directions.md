# Potential PhD Research Directions

The MSc project provides a starting point for further investigation of data-efficient temporal learning for electricity distribution networks.

Potential directions include:
1. **Federated learning for electricity-theft detection:** collaborative model training across distribution areas without centralizing raw customer data.
2. **Privacy-preserving learning:** approaches that reduce exposure of sensitive consumption information.
3. **Robust anomaly detection:** resilience to noisy, incomplete, changing, or adversarial consumption patterns.
4. **Explainable temporal models:** methods for explaining why a customer or time period is flagged.
5. **Data-efficient learning:** transfer learning, semi-supervised learning, self-supervised pretraining, and carefully evaluated augmentation.
6. **Deployment and operational evaluation:** model drift, inference constraints, human review, and integration into utility workflows.

These are prospective research directions, not completed contributions of the current MSc implementation.
