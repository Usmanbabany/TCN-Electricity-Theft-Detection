# Research Background

## Research title
**Temporal Convolutional Networks for Electricity Theft Detection With Limited Data**

## Problem context
Electricity theft is a form of non-technical loss affecting electricity distribution utilities. Detecting suspicious consumption patterns is challenging when reliable labelled customer-consumption data is limited.

This research investigates whether Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs) can learn temporal patterns useful for electricity-theft detection in limited-data settings.

## Aim
To investigate a TCN-based approach for identifying potentially suspicious electricity-consumption patterns when labelled data is limited.

## Objectives
1. Investigate TCN-based classification for electricity-theft detection.
2. Explore temporal representation and sequence preparation for consumption data.
3. Examine preprocessing and data-handling strategies for limited data.
4. Evaluate the approach using appropriate classification metrics and validation.
5. Discuss data-efficient temporal deep learning for electricity distribution networks.
